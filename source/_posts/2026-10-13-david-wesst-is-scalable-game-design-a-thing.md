<p>As familiar as I am with scalable software design for non-game development projects, in this past iteration, I started to assess whether or not scalability is something I need to consider for video game design.
</p>
<p>Video games are software, but they're not like any other software.</p>
<p>I have been dealing in software development for almost two decades at this point, but the more I tackle creating a commercial video game, the more I see how game development is just not the same.</p>
<p>My journey in game development continued with another completed iteration where game design was the focus. As the game started taking shape and a project started to form, the idea of <em>scalable game design</em> entered my mind.</p>
<p>Is scalable game design a thing?</p>
<p>This is what I reflect on in the devlog for this iteration.</p>
